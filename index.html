My work examines politics, security and society across the Middle East and South Asia, with a focus on Iran, political Islam, extremism and forced migration. I combine academic research, fieldwork and policy analysis.

For fifteen years I have also worked in the media, as an analyst on live television, a radio contributor and an op-ed writer, explaining fast-moving crises in plain language without losing their complexity.

Publications

Peer-reviewed work, policy analysis and books on Iran, regional conflict and forced migration. Each entry has a short summary.
