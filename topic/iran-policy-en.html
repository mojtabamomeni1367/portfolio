Iran-US negotiations on 3 fronts: Diplomacy under military threat and Washington’s credibility crisis

Simultaneous diplomatic messages, warnings of renewed strikes and intensified economic pressure have placed Iran-US negotiations before a decisive test. Washington is attempting to combine these instruments to change Tehran’s calculations. Yet the continuing conflict and its economic consequences are also raising costs for the United States and its partners, while casting doubt on promises that military action would quickly achieve Washington’s objectives.

2026-10-29

3 scenarios for the Tehran meeting: Mecca Pact members between averting war and mutual restraint

The simultaneous presence of senior Saudi, Pakistani and Turkish defence and security officials at the Tehran meeting has opened another channel for preventing a wider war. Mecca Pact members are pursuing talks that could support mediation while clarifying security red lines. Whether either objective advances will depend on negotiations producing commitments beyond general statements about dialogue and regional stability.
